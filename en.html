01 / LOCAL

Close to you

Core features work offline. No registration, developer cloud or automatic sync. Your imported content is not proactively uploaded.

02 / ENCRYPTED

More than hidden

Photos, videos, files and their name index are encrypted. Unlock with your own passphrase or optional Face ID.

03 / YOUR CONTROL

Backups you hold

Export an encrypted backup protected by a separate passphrase. Choose where to keep it. Restore by merging your collection.

Clear about the limits.

Importing does not delete originals. Manage copies in Photos, cloud services and older backups yourself. Exclusion from system backups is not an absolute guarantee. Screenshots, explicit unencrypted exports and compromised devices are outside the app’s protection.

Keep your passphrase and encrypted backups safe. There is no recovery backdoor.

Optional advertising, your choice

Free download. No subscriptions or in-app purchases. Optional Google AdMob app-open and rewarded ads require your opt-in; core features work with ads disabled. In Settings, complete a voluntary rewarded ad to receive 24 hours without app-open ads. The reward is granted only after confirmation; unavailable or unfinished ads give no reward. The vault locks before ads. Google may process advertising and device data as explained in the privacy policy. iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Enable or disable ads from Welcome or Settings → Advertising. Available ad privacy options let you update consent. Disabling stops future ad requests but does not delete data already processed by Google.
