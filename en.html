01 / LOCAL

Close to you

Core features work offline. No registration, developer cloud or automatic sync. Your imported content is not proactively uploaded.

02 / ENCRYPTED

More than hidden

Photos, videos, files and their name index are encrypted. Unlock with your own passphrase or optional Face ID.

03 / YOUR CONTROL

Backups you hold

Export an encrypted backup protected by a separate passphrase. Choose where to keep it. Restore by merging your collection.

Clear about the limits.

Importing does not delete originals. Manage copies in Photos, cloud services and older backups yourself. Exclusion from system backups is not an absolute guarantee. Screenshots, explicit unencrypted exports and compromised devices are outside the app’s protection.

Keep your passphrase and encrypted backups safe. There is no recovery backdoor.
