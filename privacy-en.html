Privacy policy

SealedNest Privacy Policy Effective date: 2026-10-08 Developer: Peng Lin 1. Local processing SealedNest requires no registration. The photos, videos, files, names, and folder information you select are used only for encrypted storage, viewing, and management on your device. The developer does not collect or receive this content, or receive passphrases or keys. 2. No ads or tracking The app has no ads, in-app purchases, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not build user profiles or proactively upload usage records or crash logs. 3. System permissions Photos and files are accessed through system pickers for the items you select. Camera permission is requested when taking a photo. Face ID is verified by the system; the app does not obtain facial templates. Denying optional permissions does not affect other features. 4. Backups and exports Cloud sync is not provided by default. You may choose to save encrypted backups or explicitly exported unencrypted copies elsewhere. Destination services may process and sync data independently. iOS system backups and file providers are managed by Apple or the relevant service provider. The backup-exclusion flag is not an absolute guarantee. 5. Retention and deletion Data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Deletion does not remove previously exported copies or external backups. Without usable keys and backup recovery credentials, the developer cannot restore your data. 6. External support website GitHub Pages is accessed only when you choose to open a help link. GitHub may process IP addresses and access logs under its policies. The page contains no ads, tracking, or analytics scripts added by us. Do not share private files, passphrases, or recovery credentials through public support channels. 7. Contact and policy updates Contact the developer through a GitHub Issue on the support page. Do not include private personal information in public posts. Policy updates will be published in the app and on the privacy page.
