Privacy policy

SealedNest Privacy Policy Effective date: 2026-10-19 Developer: Peng Lin 1. Local processing SealedNest requires no registration. The photos, videos, files, names, and folder information you select are used only for encrypted storage, viewing, and management on your device. The developer does not collect or receive this content, or receive passphrases or keys. 2. Optional advertising and your choices Before starting optional advertising, the app requests the native iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission when the system allows a request. The custom tracking-permission alert is not used. Declining or restricting tracking does not disable ads: the app requests non-personalized ads without IDFA access, subject to UMP consent and ad availability. Google Mobile Ads and UMP start only after the system tracking decision is resolved. Publisher first-party ID and publisher ad personalization are disabled; every ad request also specifies non-personalized treatment. We do not use fingerprinting or another identifier to bypass a tracking refusal. You can disable advertising separately in Settings → Advertising. UMP manages additional applicable privacy choices and does not replace ATT. Disabling ads stops future consent and ad requests, but does not erase data already processed by Google. A previously started SDK may finish processing already in progress. When advertising is enabled, Google may process IP addresses (to estimate general location), device or app identifiers, ads viewed and interactions, crash logs and performance diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. These data go to Google and may be shared with advertising service providers under Google's policies. We request non-personalized ads, disable the publisher first-party ID and request App Tracking Transparency authorization through iOS before starting either SDK. Non-personalized ads can still use identifiers and local storage; this is not a promise of zero collection. We do not provide vault files, filenames, passphrases or encryption keys to either SDK. The first two cold launches have no app-open ad; later app-open ads have a four-hour frequency limit. A voluntary rewarded ad in Settings grants 24 hours without app-open ads only when the SDK confirms the reward. Closing early or an unavailable ad gives no reward. The vault locks before an ad is presented. Google controls retention and deletion of data it receives under its privacy policy. You may use Google's privacy controls and contact Google regarding access or deletion rights. Disabling advertising or deleting the app does not itself delete Google's data, and the developer cannot delete it for you. For questions or applicable privacy rights concerning data handled by the developer, contact Peng Lin through the support page below; do not post sensitive information in public Issues. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Google partner data use: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites 3. System permissions Photos and files are accessed through system pickers for the items you select. Camera permission is requested when taking a photo. Face ID is verified by the system; the app does not obtain facial templates. Denying optional permissions does not affect other features. 4. Backups and exports Cloud sync is not provided by default. You may choose to save encrypted backups or explicitly exported unencrypted copies elsewhere. Destination services may process and sync data independently. iOS system backups and file providers are managed by Apple or the relevant service provider. The backup-exclusion flag is not an absolute guarantee. 5. Retention and deletion Data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Deletion does not remove previously exported copies or external backups. Without usable keys and backup recovery credentials, the developer cannot restore your data. 6. External support website GitHub Pages is accessed only when you choose to open a help link. GitHub may process IP addresses and access logs under its policies. The page contains no ads, tracking, or analytics scripts added by us. Do not share private files, passphrases, or recovery credentials through public support channels. 7. Contact and policy updates Contact the developer through a GitHub Issue on the support page. Do not include private personal information in public posts. Policy updates will be published in the app and on the privacy page.
