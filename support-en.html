Get started

  1. Create a vault with a separate passphrase of at least 12 characters. Your device must have a passcode.
  2. Tap + to select photos, videos or files, or take a photo. Check the encrypted import succeeded before managing originals.
  3. Long-press an item to rename, move or delete it. Create collections in Folders.
  4. In Settings, enable optional Face ID, change your passphrase or export an encrypted backup.

Change language

Use the globe on the welcome screen or Settings → Language. The default follows your device. Your choice applies immediately and is saved locally.

Move to another iPhone

Export an encrypted backup first. Keep its passphrase separately. Create a vault on the new device, then choose Settings → Restore encrypted backup. Check all restored items before deleting the old copy.

Forgot your passphrase?

If Face ID is still available, unlock and export an encrypted backup. Without an available unlock method, the developer cannot recover your passphrase. A usable encrypted backup and its passphrase can restore your collection into a new vault. Do not uninstall before checking your backup.

Originals, previews and exports

Importing does not delete originals from Photos or the cloud. Check Recently Deleted yourself. System pickers may download cloud originals. Photos, common videos, PDFs and UTF-8 text support preview; photo and document previews have a 64 MB limit. Other formats can be stored encrypted and explicitly exported. Unencrypted exports leave the vault’s protection. Keep the app in the foreground during imports and backups; after an interruption, unlock and check the result before retrying.
